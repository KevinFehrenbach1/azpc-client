# AZPC Forever — Auction Prices, Crafting Profit & Trading History

**AZPC Forever** is a free World of Warcraft Forever addon for auction-house research, crafting decisions and tracking trading activity.

## Features

- **Price research:** Capture auction-house observations and inspect market data available from your scans.
- **Crafting tools:** Browse known recipes, review material requirements, scan recipes and compare estimated crafting profitability when market prices are available.
- **Trading records:** Record supported auction/mail transactions, materials and completed crafts for your trading history.
- **In-game utilities:** Minimap menu, Getting Started, Sync Status, settings and an in-game bug reporter with optional diagnostic details.
- **Optional web dashboard:** Pair the addon with the AZPC Launcher and Watcher to upload supported saved records and use the private dashboard at https://forever.azpc.market/.

## Getting started

Install the addon through CurseForge, log in and open the **AZPC** minimap menu. Select **Getting Started**. For dashboard syncing, visit **https://azpc.market** to install the separate Windows launcher and watcher. The addon can run without the watcher; dashboard syncing cannot.

## Important beta notes

This is beta software. Auction-house prices may be missing or stale until scans are collected. Estimated profits are not guaranteed sale proceeds. Some trading events may require opening the mailbox and reloading to save records. Not all costs can be reconstructed for historical materials. The watcher is an optional, separate Windows component and is **not bundled** in the CurseForge ZIP.
