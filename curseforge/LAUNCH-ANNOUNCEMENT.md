## Longer announcement (website / forum)

# Introducing AZPC Forever

Auction-house decisions are easier when you can see more than one listing. AZPC Forever is an independent addon built to help players research auction prices, review crafting materials and understand their own trading history.

### What you can do
- Capture supported auction-house observations while browsing.
- Explore recipes, required materials and estimated crafting profitability when pricing is available.
- Track supported mailbox transactions, materials and completed crafts.
- Open Getting Started, Sync Status and Report Bug directly from the minimap menu.
- Optionally connect the separate Windows AZPC Watcher to upload saved data and use private web dashboards.

### Get started
Install the **AZPC Forever** addon for the verified Forever game client through CurseForge. In game, open the AZPC minimap menu and select **Getting Started**. For optional web syncing, visit **https://azpc.market** for the separate launcher and watcher.

### Help us improve
AZPC is in beta. Price observations vary by realm, faction and scan coverage. Historical material costs may be incomplete. If something looks wrong, use **Report Bug** and review the diagnostic preview before sharing.

AZPC is an independent project and is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
